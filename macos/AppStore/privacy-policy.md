# RK 原生烧录工具隐私政策

更新日期：2026-08-31

RK 原生烧录工具不要求注册账号，不包含广告、分析或跟踪组件，也不向开发者或第三方服务器
发送固件内容、文件路径、设备标识、使用记录或诊断日志。

## Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: August 31, 2026

RK Native Flash Tool does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking
components. It does not transmit firmware content, file paths, device identifiers, usage records, or
diagnostic logs to the developer or third-party servers.

The app accesses local firmware files only after the user selects them and communicates with a connected
Rockchip device over USB. Processing occurs locally between the Mac and the target device. Extracted
firmware caches and configuration files are stored in the app sandbox or a user-selected location and can
be removed by the user.

macOS and the Mac App Store may process download, purchase, crash, or store analytics data under Apple's
own privacy policies. That data is not collected directly by the app developer.
